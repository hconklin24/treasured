It notices

Just write. Treasured does the remembering.

After you write, Treasured picks out who you mentioned, what's weighing on you, what you're thankful for, and the prayer requests hiding in your words, ready to keep with one tap. No tagging. No forms.

It connects

Today's entry, linked to what you wrote months ago.

When the same person or the same worry comes back, Treasured shows you the thread: what you wrote, what you prayed, and what happened since.

It remembers

Every prayer, and how it was answered.

Keep requests for yourself and others, add updates as life unfolds, and mark them answered in your own words. Treasured brings quiet prayers back to mind, and reminds you of what God has done.

Scripture in context

Scripture, not scripts.

For what you're carrying, Treasured shows related passages from the full World English Bible: always the real text with the verses around it, never computer-written. It points you to Scripture and to your people. It doesn't tell you what God is saying.

Reflect

See what keeps coming up.

Weekly and monthly reflections gather the themes, the people on your mind, and your gratitude, all in your own words.

A journal, not a chatbot.

Treasured won't tell you what God is saying or interpret your life for you. It notices what you wrote, points you to Scripture, and encourages you to pray for others and share what you're carrying with your church, your pastor, and the people you trust.
