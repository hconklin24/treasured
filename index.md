# The prayer journal that remembers.

Treasured notices the people, burdens, and prayers in what you write, connects them across months, and brings back what's worth revisiting — privately, on your phone.

> "But Mary kept all these sayings, pondering them in her heart." — Luke 2:19 (WEB)

- **It notices.** Just write. Treasured picks out who you mentioned, what's weighing on you, and the prayer requests in your words.
- **It remembers.** Today's entry connects to what you wrote months ago. Answered prayers are remembered.
- **It stays yours.** Everything happens on your device, encrypted, and works in airplane mode.
- **Scripture in context.** Related passages from the World English Bible, never computer-written.
